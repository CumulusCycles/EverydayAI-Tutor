EverydayAI Tutor takes viewers on a structured learning path from absolute beginner to confident AI user. No technical background is required at any stage.

The journey is built around six content areas, covered in sequence:

Understand AI — What AI actually is, how it works, and why it matters, explained in plain English with no assumed knowledge.

Use AI Tools — Hands-on guidance with tools like ChatGPT and Claude that anyone can start using today, regardless of background.

Master Prompting — How to communicate with AI effectively so you get dramatically better results. Prompting is a skill, and this pillar teaches it step by step.

Real-World Workflows — Practical use cases for everyday life and work. This is about using AI to get real things done, not just experimenting.

Going Deeper — Advanced prompting, automation, and agentic AI for viewers who want to push further after mastering the basics.

Build with AI — Claude Code, AI-assisted development, and building real software projects using AI tools. This is where the channel meets Rob's technical background and shows what is possible when AI is used as a development partner.

The channel is designed so that a complete beginner can start at step one and follow the path at their own pace. Each stage builds naturally on the previous one, and the goal is always the same: confident, practical use of AI in real life.
