Rob Frenette is the host and creator of EverydayAI Tutor. He is a full-stack developer, cloud engineer, and educator with a passion for making complex technology accessible to everyday people.

By day Rob designs and builds full-stack applications deployed on AWS cloud infrastructure. He is an AWS Community Builder in AI Engineering, which means he stays at the edge of what is possible with cloud and artificial intelligence.

Rob also runs Cumulus Cycles, a YouTube channel covering AWS cloud development and AI engineering tutorials aimed at developers and engineers. That channel is at https://www.youtube.com/@CumulusCycles.

EverydayAI Tutor came from a simple observation: AI is transforming how people work and live, but most educational content about AI assumes you are already technical. Rob created EverydayAI Tutor for everyone else — people who want honest, practical guidance without jargon or hype, from someone who builds with these tools every day.

Rob believes the best way to learn something deeply is to teach it clearly. That belief is at the core of every video and piece of content on the channel.

Rob's LinkedIn profile is at https://www.linkedin.com/in/robertmfrenette.
