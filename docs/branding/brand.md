# EverydayAI Tutor — Brand Guide
# For Development Use

---

## Brand Overview

**Brand Name:** EverydayAI Tutor
**Tagline:** Practical AI for Everyday People
**Subtitle:** From Curious Beginner to Confident AI User
**Website:** AIEverydayTutor.com
**YouTube Channel:** https://www.youtube.com/@EverydayAITutor

---

## Mission

To make AI accessible, practical, and approachable for everyday people — with zero technical background required.

EverydayAI Tutor takes complete beginners from understanding what AI is all the way to confidently using and eventually building with AI tools.

---

## Target Audience

- Complete beginners with no technical background
- People who have heard about AI constantly but feel intimidated
- Curious non-technical adults who want practical skills, not theory
- People who may have tried ChatGPT once or twice but don't know where to go next
- Professionals who want to use AI to improve their productivity
- Anyone who feels left behind by the AI revolution and wants to catch up

### What They Are NOT
- Developers or programmers
- Computer science students
- Early adopters or tech enthusiasts
- People looking for hype or futurism

---

## Brand Personality

### Voice & Tone
- Warm and approachable — like a knowledgeable friend, not a professor
- Confident but never intimidating
- Encouraging and empowering
- Practical and grounded — always focused on real-world value
- Honest — including about AI's limitations
- Conversational, never corporate
- Modern without being trendy or buzzword-heavy

### What the Brand Sounds Like
- "You don't need to be technical to use AI effectively."
- "Let's make this simple."
- "Here's what this actually means for you."
- "Most people don't know this — but you will."

### What the Brand Does NOT Sound Like
- Fear-based or doom-focused ("AI is taking over...")
- Overhyped or breathless ("AI will change EVERYTHING!")
- Technical or jargon-heavy
- Corporate or cold
- Condescending or oversimplified

## Visual Identity

### Logo
- Circular format with warm beige background
- Orange chat bubble icon (three dots) above the wordmark
- "Everyday" and "Tutor" in deep navy bold sans-serif
- "AI" in accent orange bold sans-serif
- Orange underline accent beneath the wordmark

### Color Palette

#### Primary Colors
| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Soft White Background | `#F7F5F2` |
| Deep Navy Headline | `#0F172A` |
| Charcoal Gray Text | `#334155` |
| Accent Orange | `#F97316` |
| Subtle UI Accent Blue | `#5B6EF5` |
| Light Gray Divider | `#E5E7EB` |

#### Supporting Colors
| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Success Green | `#22C55E` |
| Warm Beige Accent | `#EADCCB` |
| Soft Workspace Gray | `#D6D9DE` |
| Muted Slate Blue | `#64748B` |

#### Usage Ratios
- Soft Whites & Light Grays: 60–70%
- Navy & Charcoal Typography: 20–25%
- Orange Accent Highlights: 5–8%
- Supporting UI Colors: 5%

### Typography
- **Style:** Bold geometric sans-serif for headlines; medium-weight modern sans-serif for body
- **Recommended Families:** Poppins, Sora, Manrope, or Inter
- **Headline:** Bold, high contrast, navy or orange
- **Body:** Charcoal gray, clean and readable

### Visual Style
- Clean, modern, and warm — never cold or corporate
- Light backgrounds dominate; white and soft off-white surfaces
- Orange used sparingly for high-impact emphasis only
- Subtle dot patterns and soft organic shapes for background texture
- Rounded corners, friendly card-based UI components
- No dark cyberpunk aesthetics
- No neon colors
- No robot or sci-fi imagery
- No overwhelming technical diagrams

### Imagery Direction
- Real people — approachable, relatable, everyday settings
- People at laptops in warm, bright, home or office environments
- Expressions of curiosity, confidence, and engagement
- UI mockup cards and simple diagrams overlaid on imagery
- Warm, natural lighting

---

## Brand Positioning

### What EverydayAI Tutor IS
- An educational YouTube channel and web platform
- A structured learning journey from absolute beginner to confident AI user
- A trusted, honest guide through the AI landscape
- Practical skills for real life — not theory for its own sake

### What EverydayAI Tutor is NOT
- A news or commentary channel about AI
- A developer tutorial channel
- A hype channel
- A corporate training product

### Key Differentiators
- Genuinely beginner-friendly — no assumed knowledge
- Warm and human, not technical and cold
- Honest about AI's limitations as well as its strengths
- Structured journey — not random disconnected videos
- Practical outcome focus — viewers learn to DO things with AI

---

## Content Pillars

1. **Understanding AI** — What it is, how it works, why it matters
2. **Using AI Tools** — Hands-on with ChatGPT, Claude, and others
3. **Prompting Skills** — How to communicate with AI effectively
4. **Real-World Workflows** — Practical productivity and everyday use cases
5. **Going Deeper** — Advanced prompting, automation, agentic AI
6. **Building with AI** — Eventually: Claude Code and AI-assisted development

---

## Emotional Promise

A viewer who finds EverydayAI Tutor should feel:
- Relieved — "Finally, someone explaining this in a way I can understand."
- Empowered — "I can actually do this."
- Curious — "I want to learn more."
- Confident — "I'm not as far behind as I thought."
