## Palette Style Notes

- Bright, modern, approachable educational branding
- Human-centered AI education aesthetic
- Designed to make AI feel approachable and non-intimidating
- Warm, trustworthy, beginner-friendly tone
- Clean visual hierarchy with emotionally approachable design
- Strong typography contrast optimized for YouTube readability
- Avoids harsh neon, dark cyberpunk aesthetics, or overly technical visuals
- Uses orange sparingly for high-impact emphasis

---

## Emotional Tone

- Warm and approachable
- Confident but not intimidating
- Helpful rather than futuristic
- Modern without cyberpunk aesthetics
- Educational, trustworthy, and practical

---

## Typography Pairing Recommendation

- **Headline Font Style:** Bold geometric sans-serif
- **Subtitle Font Style:** Medium-weight modern sans-serif
- **Recommended Families:** Inter, Poppins, Manrope, or Sora

---

## Suggested Usage Ratios

| Color Group | Suggested Usage |
|---|---|
| Soft Whites & Light Grays | 60–70% |
| Navy & Charcoal Typography | 20–25% |
| Orange Accent Highlights | 5–8% |
| Supporting UI Colors | 5% |

---

## Brand Positioning Alignment

This palette is designed to support the EverydayAI Tutor brand identity:

- AI education for everyday people
- Beginner-friendly without feeling simplistic
- Modern and polished without feeling corporate
- Practical rather than futuristic
- Emotionally warm, trustworthy, and approachable

The overall aesthetic should feel more like a modern educational platform or premium productivity brand than a stereotypical “AI tech” company.
